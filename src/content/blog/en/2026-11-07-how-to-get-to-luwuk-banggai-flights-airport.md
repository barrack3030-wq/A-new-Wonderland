Almost every Banggai Islands trip starts in one place: Syukuran Aminuddin Amir Airport in Luwuk, code LUW. From here you reach the waterfalls, the beaches and the harbor where boats leave for the islands. From outside Central Sulawesi, the usual route is to fly via Makassar, and since March 2026 there has also been a direct flight from Jakarta. Airline routes and schedules change often, so treat the figures below as a guide and check again before you buy a ticket.

## Luwuk airport: location and distance to town

The airport is in South Luwuk, Banggai Regency, and according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syukuran_Aminuddin_Amir_Airport) it lies about 10 km from the town center. Another travel guide puts the drive at around 15 minutes. In practice you can be at your guesthouse quickly unless traffic is heavy.

## Routes available

| Surabaya, Kendari, Palu | Indonesia AirAsia | Since 7 March 2026 via Makassar as a hub, according to an [AirAsia release](https://newsroom.airasia.com/news/2026/2/20/indonesia-airasia-hubungkan-luwuk-ke-surabaya-kendari-dan-palu-via-makassar) |

According to that report, the Jakarta to Luwuk flight leaves around 03:00 WIB and lands around 07:20 WITA, while the return leaves around 08:00 WITA. A morning arrival gives you almost a full day in Luwuk. A morning departure home, on the other hand, means your last night is best spent in town rather than on an island.

## From Bali and elsewhere

There is no direct flight from Bali to Luwuk. The usual route is Bali to Makassar, then Makassar to Luwuk. The same applies to international visitors: there are no direct international flights to Luwuk, so a connection in Makassar or Jakarta is unavoidable. If you are weighing Banggai as a follow-up to Bali, our [guide to Bali alternatives](/en/blog/2026-09-16-luwuk-and-the-banggai-islands-a-quieter-wilder-alternative-to-bali/) explains how the two compare.

## The Makassar connection: where most problems happen

The most common mistake is not the flight itself but the layover. A few notes:

- **Leave enough time.** One travel guide we read suggests at least about three hours for a domestic connection and longer if you arrive on an international flight. We agree: waiting at the airport is better than losing a day.
- **Separate tickets are not automatically linked.** If you buy two tickets on different airlines, you may have to collect and re-check your bags, and a delay on the first flight is your own risk.
- **Choose a morning flight.** An earlier Makassar to Luwuk flight leaves room for delays and gets you in early enough to sort out your accommodation.
- **Fares tend to rise close to departure.** One local guide gives a range of roughly IDR 800,000 to 1,500,000 one way for Makassar to Luwuk. Treat that as an estimate, not a quote.

## Haze and cancelled flights

One risk worth planning for is visibility. On 15 October 2026, Batik Air [announced cancellations](https://www.lionair.co.id/tentang-kami/newsroom/2026/10/15/media-statementdiperbarui-15-oktober-2026-pukul-16.00-wita-penerbangan-batik-air-dari-ke-bandar-udara-syukuran-aminuddin-amir-luwuk-terdampak-kabut-asap) of several Makassar-Luwuk and Luwuk-Makassar flights because haze reduced visibility around the airport. The airline offered refunds or rebooking under its terms. This is not routine, but it is reason enough to leave a spare day before a return flight or an important connection. We discuss the effect on travel in our article on [the Luwuk haze and Banggai tourism](/en/blog/2026-10-16-luwuk-haze-impact-banggai-tourism/).

## After you land: what to do in Luwuk

- **Withdraw cash in Luwuk.** On the islands, ATMs are rare or absent. Our preparation guide explains [how to plan cash and other essentials](/en/blog/2026-10-20-banggai-trip-preparation-checklist/).
- **Boats leave from town, not from the airport.** Island boats depart from the harbor in Luwuk, generally in the afternoon. If you land in the late afternoon, plan an overnight stay in Luwuk. Route details are in our guide on [how to get to the Banggai Islands from Luwuk](/en/blog/2026-09-18-how-to-get-to-banggai-islands-from-luwuk/).
- **Get a local SIM if you need one.** Another travel guide says Telkomsel has the best coverage in this area. Check the signal for your own destination.

## How to use your first day

**If you arrive in the morning (for example on the direct Jakarta flight).** Drop your bags at your accommodation, then use the afternoon for a stop near town such as [Piala Waterfall](/en/destinations/piala-waterfall/) or [Salodik Waterfall](/en/destinations/salodik-waterfall/). The full plan is in our [2-day Luwuk itinerary](/en/blog/2026-11-02-luwuk-2-days-1-night-itinerary-waterfall-coast/). The next morning you can continue to the islands without rushing.

**If you arrive in the afternoon.** Rest, sort out cash, and confirm the boat or transfer for the next day. Do not force the first day to be a sightseeing day.

For any trip that involves boats, keep one spare day at the end. We explain why in our [weather backup plan for island hopping](/en/blog/2026-10-23-banggai-island-hopping-weather-backup-plan/).
