Luwuk and the Banggai Islands in Sulawesi offer beaches, islands, and beautiful seascapes. Getting between islands, however, may require a combination of road and boat travel rather than flights alone. A few checks before departure can help you avoid last-minute changes.

## 1. Leave a buffer between flights and onward travel

If you plan to continue by road or board a boat soon after arriving in Luwuk, make sure the timing is realistic. Flight delays, baggage collection, and road conditions can all take longer than expected. When a boat departure is fixed, avoid tight connections and consider staying overnight in Luwuk if needed.

## 2. Reconfirm boat schedules close to your travel date

Inter-island boats and local services can operate differently depending on the route and date. Do not rely solely on a timetable found online. As your trip approaches, confirm the departure time, port, and arrival point with your accommodation or a local operator. Leave extra time in case weather or operating conditions change.

## 3. Prepare cash

Before leaving Luwuk, set aside Indonesian rupiah for accommodation, meals, local transport, boat transfers, and entrance fees. ATMs and card payments may not be available on smaller islands or in villages. Estimate your expenses with a reserve and keep your cash securely in separate places.

## 4. Check how you will reach your accommodation

Your arrival point may be some distance from your accommodation and could require another boat or vehicle. Before booking, ask for the exact drop-off location, whether pickup is available, and whether boat transfers are included in the room rate. If you expect to arrive late, confirm pickup hours as well.

## 5. Adapt sea activities to the conditions

Before snorkeling or taking a boat tour, ask a local guide about the weather, waves, currents, and visibility. Safety matters more than completing every stop on an itinerary. Beginners should go with an experienced local operator and use the recommended safety equipment.

## 6. Pack the essentials

- A hat and lightweight long-sleeved clothing for sun protection
- A dry bag and a waterproof phone case
- Personal medication and supplies for motion sickness
- A reusable water bottle and a small bag for carrying out rubbish
- Booking details and accommodation contacts available offline
- Cash and a copy of your identification

Pack according to your travel style and accommodation. Prioritize what you need rather than bringing too much.

## 7. Respect local communities and the marine environment

Ask permission before photographing residents or their homes, and respect local customs and privacy. Do not step on or touch coral, remove marine life, or leave rubbish behind. Follow conservation rules and your local guide’s instructions.

## Final checks before departure

- Is there enough time between your flight and the next connection?
- Have you confirmed the boat operating day, departure port, and arrival point?
- Do you know how to reach your accommodation and arrange pickup?
- Have you prepared cash and personal essentials?
- Do you have an alternative plan if the weather deteriorates?

Travel around Banggai is more comfortable when connections are not scheduled too tightly. Leave a buffer and confirm current details with your airline, accommodation, or boat operator before departure.

Flight and boat schedules can change with the date and local conditions. Always confirm the latest information directly with service providers before booking.
