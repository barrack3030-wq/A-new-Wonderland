Clear lagoons and quiet island beaches make it tempting to book a boat trip as soon as you see the photos. But island hopping in the Banggai region is also a logistics decision: the starting port, land transfers, boat availability, local conditions and package inclusions can shape the day as much as the destinations themselves.

Before paying a deposit, ask these seven questions and get the answers in writing.

## 1. Where exactly does the trip start and finish?

Confirm the meeting point, pickup time, departure port and drop-off location. Luwuk is on mainland Sulawesi, while several Banggai attractions are on other islands. Do not assume hotel pickup or a return to the same place is included unless the operator confirms it.

Ask which legs use a car, speedboat, local boat or ferry, where applicable.

## 2. Which destinations are actually included?

Request a written itinerary, not just a promotional collage. Check the names of the stops, their order and the time planned at each location. If Paisupok, [Paisu Batango](/en/destinations/paisu-batango-lake/), [Kawalu Bay](/en/destinations/kawalu-bay/) or [Poganda Beach](/en/destinations/poganda-beach/) matters to you, ask whether each place is included in your specific package and whether the route works on your travel date.

A destination shown in advertising is not automatically part of every tour.

## 3. What does the quoted price cover?

Ask whether the price includes the boat, fuel, land transport, driver, entrance fees, guide, meals, drinking water and local charges. Check what happens to the price if the group size changes or you request a different route.

Compare quotes on a like-for-like basis rather than choosing the lowest headline price.

## 4. What happens if sea or weather conditions change?

Boat travel depends on local conditions. Ask who makes the safety decision, whether the route may change, and what the rescheduling or refund policy is if the boat cannot operate.

Be cautious of promises that every stop is guaranteed regardless of conditions.

## 5. How long are the transfers between stops?

Ask for realistic estimates for road and sea legs, including waiting time and meal breaks. A route that looks short on a map can take longer when it involves multiple transfers.

If you have a flight or ferry to catch afterwards, tell the operator before agreeing to the schedule. Avoid tight connections after a day on the water.

## 6. What should you bring, and what local rules apply?

Confirm whether you should bring cash, drinking water, food, a change of clothes, sun protection, suitable footwear and a dry bag. Ask about swimming, snorkelling, drone use and site-specific rules. Do not assume reliable mobile coverage, card payments or supply shops will be available in remote areas.

Follow local guidance and take your rubbish with you.

## 7. Who is your contact on the day?

Get a contact name or number, the confirmed pickup time, a recognisable meeting point and instructions for schedule changes. Save your booking details and accommodation address offline.

Before transferring money, double-check the date, group size, agreed services, deposit, balance and cancellation terms in writing.

## A quick pre-payment checklist

- [ ] Exact meeting point and end point confirmed.
- [ ] Stops and route order written down.
- [ ] Price inclusions and exclusions explained.
- [ ] Weather and cancellation policy understood.
- [ ] Transfer times look realistic.
- [ ] Equipment and local rules confirmed.
- [ ] Operator contact and booking proof saved.

## Choose a trip that fits your pace

The best Banggai itinerary is not necessarily the one with the most stops. It is the one that gives you time to enjoy each place while allowing for safe travel and realistic transfers. If you are starting in Luwuk, share your dates, group size and priority destinations before requesting a quote.
