# Banggai Honeymoon: 4 Days 3 Nights for 2 People

Banggai is a great choice for couples looking for a quieter island escape. The **Banggai Honeymoon Escape** is designed as a private 4-day, 3-night trip for two.

The route connects Luwuk, Salakan, and several Banggai coastal destinations including **Paisu Pok, Paisu Batango, Kawalu Bay, Poganda Beach, Long Beach, Oyama, Bontolan, Pompon Beach, and Mandel Beach**.

## 4 Days 3 Nights Itinerary

| Day | Route | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Luwuk → Salakan | Piala Waterfall · Salakan |
| **Day 2** | Banggai Island | Paisu Pok · Paisu Batango · Kawalu Bay · Poganda Beach |
| **Day 3** | Coastal Escape | Long Beach · Oyama · Bontolan |
| **Day 4** | Salakan → Luwuk | Pompon Beach · Mandel Beach · Return to Luwuk |

### Day 1 — Luwuk → Salakan

Arrive in Luwuk and begin the journey with a visit to **Piala Waterfall** before continuing to Salakan.

After arriving in Salakan, check in and rest before the next day's island adventure.

### Day 2 — Paisu Pok & Banggai Coast

Day two is one of the main highlights of the trip, covering **Paisu Pok, Paisu Batango, Kawalu Bay, and Poganda Beach**.

Enjoy time for swimming when conditions are safe, photography, relaxing by the beach, and taking in Banggai's coastal scenery.

### Day 3 — Hidden Beaches & Coastal Views

Continue to **Long Beach, Oyama, and Bontolan**.

The day is kept relaxed so couples can enjoy the beaches and coastal scenery without rushing between destinations.

### Day 4 — Salakan → Luwuk

Before returning to Luwuk, visit **Pompon Beach and Mandel Beach**.

In the afternoon, travel back to Luwuk for your onward flight or next journey.
