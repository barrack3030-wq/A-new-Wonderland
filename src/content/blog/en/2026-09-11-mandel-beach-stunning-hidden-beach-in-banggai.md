**Discover Hidden Paradise of Banggai**

Imagine waking up to the sound of gentle waves, fine white sand beneath your feet, and turquoise sea water so clear you can see all the way to the bottom. No hustle and bustle, no photo queues, just you and the untouched beauty of nature. Welcome to **Mandel Beach**—a hidden gem in Banggai that has slipped under the radar of mainstream tourists until now.

Located between Kombutokan Village and Palam Village, Totikum District, Banggai Kepulauan Regency, Central Sulawesi, Mandel Beach is the perfect definition of the *hidden paradise* you've been looking for. Featuring a coastline of about 2 kilometers, this beach offers a stretch of soft white sand, clear light blue seawater, and is surrounded by lush green grassy hills and shady trees that treat the eyes.

Unlike popular beaches in Bali or Lombok that are already crowded and commercialized, Mandel Beach is still very natural and untouched by major development. This is the right destination for you—adventure travelers, nature lovers, photographers, and couples looking to enjoy tranquility without interruption.


## Where Exactly is Mandel Beach?

Mandel Beach is located on **Peling Island** (often spelled Peleng), one of the main islands in Banggai Kepulauan Regency, Central Sulawesi. This island can be accessed via Luwuk City as the main gateway to the Banggai area.

Administratively, the beach is located in the area of **Palam Village, Totikum District**, although some sources also mention it being situated between Kombutokan Village and Palam Village. What is clear is that its location lies in the northern coastal area of Peling Island, which is still very pristine.

**GPS Coordinates:** Latitude -1.2731592, Longitude 123.5309306

## How to Get to Mandel Beach

The journey to Mandel Beach is part of the adventure itself. Here is the full route:

### 1. Fly to Luwuk
The first step is to fly to **Syukuran Aminuddin Amir Airport (LUW)** in Luwuk. Flights are available from Makassar, Jakarta, and Bali, usually with a transit in Makassar. Flight duration from Makassar takes around 1-2 hours.

### 2. Ferry from Luwuk to Peling Island
From **Luwuk Public Port**, you can take a public ferry to **Leme Leme** on Peling Island. This ferry departs daily around **2:00 PM** and takes about **2 hours**. Ferry tickets are around IDR 54,000 per person.

**Tip:** Sit near the front of the boat to avoid engine smoke and enjoy the stunning ocean views.

### 3. Overland Journey to Mandel Beach
From Leme Leme or Salakan, you can rent a motorcycle or car to travel to Mandel Beach. The journey from Salakan takes about **45 minutes to 1 hour** along the island's northern coastal road.

Another alternative is to rent a motorized boat from Banggai Port, with a travel time of around 1-2 hours depending on sea conditions.

**Easy Access:** This beach can be reached by both 4-wheel and 2-wheel vehicles, making it quite flexible for various types of travelers. From the parking area, it only requires a short walk of less than 200 meters to reach the shoreline.


## Exciting Activities at Mandel Beach

### 1. Snorkeling and Diving
This is the main attraction of Mandel Beach. Its clear seawater and gentle waves make it a paradise for snorkelers and divers. The coral reefs in Mandel waters are still in very good condition, both large and small, serving as a home to a variety of cute, colorful fish species.

For those bringing your own snorkeling gear, this is a golden opportunity to explore the untouched underwater beauty of Banggai. If you do not bring gear, you can rent it from local residents or through tour operators.

### 2. Swimming and Relaxing
With calm waves and clear water, Mandel Beach is very safe for swimming, even for families with children. You can swim freely, play in the sand, or simply relax under coconut trees while enjoying the sea breeze.

### 3. Enjoying Sunrise and Sunset
One of the most magical moments at Mandel Beach is witnessing the **sunrise**. This beach faces east, so you can watch a mesmerizing sunrise panorama from behind the green hills. For those who want to camp, spending the night on the beach and greeting the morning sunrise is an unforgettable experience.

### 4. Photography
With a backdrop of white sand, turquoise water, and green hills, Mandel Beach is a paradise for photographers. Every corner offers a perfect composition for landscape, portrait, and underwater photography.

### 5. Island Hopping
Use Mandel Beach as a base to explore other destinations on Peling Island, such as **Lake Paisu Pok** famous for its glass-like blue water, **Poganda Beach**, and **Piala Waterfall**.


## When is the Best Time to Visit?

**The dry season (May–October)** is the best time to visit Mandel Beach. During this period, the weather is clearer, the sea is calmer, and underwater visibility for snorkeling is much better. If you want to enjoy the beach in its best condition, avoid the rainy season which usually runs from November to April.

To avoid crowds, visit the beach on weekdays. Although Mandel Beach is not yet crowded, weekends and national holidays are usually visited by more local tourists.


## Practical Tips for Travelers

### Facilities Available
Please note that **Mandel Beach does not yet have adequate facilities**. There are no public toilets, food stalls, or accommodations at the beach location. A few gazebos exist, but their condition is unmaintained. Therefore, you need to prepare yourself well.

### What to Bring
- Sufficient **drinking water and food** (no stalls on location)
- **Sunscreen** and **insect repellent**
- Your own **snorkeling gear** (if you wish to snorkel)
- **Cash** (ATMs are very limited outside main towns)
- **Camera** and **drone** to capture the moments
- **Tent** if you plan to camp

### Entrance Fee
The entrance ticket to Mandel Beach is very affordable, at **IDR 10,000 per person** (prices are subject to change at any time).

### Accommodation
Since there is no accommodation around Mandel Beach, you can stay in **Salakan** (the capital of Banggai Kepulauan Regency) or in **Leme Leme**. Some accommodation options include:
- **Permata 55 Homestay** (budget)
- **Kota Hotel Luwuk** (mid-range)
- Local homestays starting from IDR 100,000 per night

### Local Transportation
- **Motorcycle rental:** IDR 100,000–180,000 per day
- **Car rental with driver:** around IDR 1,000,000 for long-distance trips
- **Luwuk–Leme Leme public ferry:** IDR 54,000 per person
