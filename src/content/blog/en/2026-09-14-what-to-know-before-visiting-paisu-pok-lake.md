Paisu Pok Lake has long been a topic of conversation among travelers who follow Indonesian travel accounts. Its crystal-clear water, submerged tree trunks visible from the surface, and location on a remote island make this lake feel like a destination accessible only to those who truly intend to visit. The photos are indeed stunning, but the journey there requires more preparation than simply buying a plane ticket.

This article is not about why you should go. This article is about **what you need to know before you go**—from how to reach the lake, the costs you need to prepare for, the rules in place on site, to the small details that are often missed by first-time visitors to Banggai.

### Where Is Paisu Pok Lake?

Paisu Pok Lake is located in **Luk Panenteng Village, North Bulagi District, Banggai Kepulauan Regency, Central Sulawesi**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1). This lake is situated on **Peleng Island**, the largest island in the Banggai Archipelago. From Leme-Leme port on Peleng, the lake is approximately **23 kilometers** away[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/).

Luk Panenteng Village is the most practical base for staying overnight. From homestays around the village, the lake can be reached on foot in about **5–10 minutes** via a paved path[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1).

### How to Get to Paisu Pok Lake

The journey to Paisu Pok Lake involves several stages, and each stage has its own characteristics.

#### Step 1: Fly to Luwuk

Syukuran Aminuddin Amir Airport (LUW) in Luwuk operates daily direct flights from **Jakarta** and **Makassar**. If you are coming from Bali or another city, a transit in one of those cities is usually required[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/). From the airport to central Luwuk City, a taxi ride takes about **20 minutes** and costs around **50,000 IDR**[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/).

#### Step 2: Cross to Peleng Island

There are two main ways to cross to Peleng:

**Public ferry.** The ferry departs from **Pelabuhan Rakyat Luwuk** daily around **2:00 PM** and arrives in **Leme-Leme, Peleng** about **2 hours** later. Tickets are bought on-site for around **54,000 IDR** (approx. 3 USD) per person. This ferry is a large wooden boat with a capacity of 50–100 passengers, equipped with simple bunks and benches. If you want more privacy, you can rent a small fan-cooled room for two people for an extra **50,000 IDR**[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/). There is no online booking, but tickets usually do not sell out[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/). The ferry is often delayed by about an hour—something to anticipate if you have a tight schedule.

**Private speedboat.** A faster alternative is renting a speedboat from Luwuk. The journey takes about **1 hour**, and you can sometimes spot dolphins along the way. The charter cost for a speedboat ranges between **1.5 to 4 million IDR** (approx. 100–250 USD) round-trip, depending on the size and route. This can be an economical option if you are traveling in a group and sharing costs[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/).

#### Step 3: From Leme-Leme to Luk Panenteng

After arriving in Peleng, the overland trip to Luk Panenteng is still about **23 kilometers**. You can rent a scooter from **Novpitri Homestay** near the port, or hire a car with a driver. The road to the village is partially paved, but there are also damaged sections. This journey takes about **1 hour**[](https://theworldtravelguy.com/paisu-pok-lake-banggai/).

If you choose the route via **Salakan** (the capital of Banggai Kepulauan Regency), the land trip from Salakan to Paisu Pok takes about **2 hours** with a car rental costing around **400,000 IDR** (excluding gasoline and driver)[](https://banggairaya.id/berkunjung-ke-danau-paisu-pok-banggai-kepulauan-rute-dan-budget-yang-harus-disiapkan-dari-luwuk/).

### Entrance Fees and On-Site Costs

Entrance fees to Paisu Pok Lake are relatively affordable, but there are several cost items you should know about.

 - **Lake entrance ticket:** around **5,000–10,000 IDR** per person. Some sources mention different figures, so prepare cash in small denominations[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1).
 - **Motorcycle parking:** around **3,000–5,000 IDR**
 - **Car parking:** around **10,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)
 - **Canoe rental:** around **30,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)
 - **Paddleboard rental:** around **100,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)
 - **Snorkeling gear rental:** around **50,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)
 - **Gazebo rental:** around **30,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)
 - **Overnight camping:** around **35,000 IDR**[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1)

These prices may change over time, especially as visitor numbers increase. It is advisable to **bring sufficient cash** as ATMs are very limited on Peleng[](https://luwukbanggai.com/penginapan-kepulauan-banggai/).

### What Facilities Are Available?

Paisu Pok Lake is equipped with several basic facilities, although not as complete as commercial tourist destinations. You will find **docks, toilets, changing rooms, fairly neat walkways, boats, and several gazebos** for resting[](https://www.navaswara.com/read/2026/08/31/tersembunyi-di-banggai-danau-paisu-pok-punya-air-sebening-kaca/#main#1). Around the lake, **there are no food vendors**, so bringing your own meals/snacks is highly recommended.

### What to Bring

Several items you should prepare before leaving:

 - **Cash in small denominations.** ATMs are limited, and not all places accept non-cash payments.
 - **A change of clothes.** If you plan to swim or snorkel.
 - **Comfortable, non-slip footwear.** The area around the lake can be slippery, especially after rain.
 - **Sunscreen and sun protection.** The lake area is quite exposed.
 - **Insect repellent.** Especially if visiting during the rainy season.
 - **Drinking water and snacks.** There are no food vendors around the lake.
 - **Camera or phone.** This lake is very photogenic, and you will not want to miss it.
 - **Life jacket if needed.** If you are not confident swimming in open water.

### Safety and Environmental Rules

Paisu Pok Lake has varying depths, ranging from **5 to 15 meters** depending on the location. Some sources mention depths of 4–5 meters in certain areas, while other areas can reach up to 15 meters. If you wish to swim, make sure you **can truly swim at such depths**. For extra safety, a life jacket can be a wise choice.

There are also environmental rules that must be followed. This lake is a **freshwater lake** that serves as a vital water source for local villagers. **Do not use soap or any chemical products in or on the edge of the lake**. If you use sunscreen or insect repellent, use it sparingly and avoid applying it right before entering the water. These products can pollute the water and disrupt the lake's ecosystem.

Pack out your trash. There is no modern waste management system around the lake, and keeping the lake clean is a shared responsibility.

### Best Time to Visit

The dry season in Central Sulawesi lasts from **April to November**, and this is the best period to visit Paisu Pok Lake[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1). During this season, rain is less frequent, the water is clearer, and road access is better. The rainy season (November–March) can still be visited, but the risks include cloudier water and disrupted ferry schedules.

For the best single-day experience, **midday to afternoon** is often recommended because direct sunlight hits the water's surface and brings out the most vivid blue color[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1). However, if you stay overnight in Luk Panenteng, early morning before day-trippers arrive is the quietest time.

Avoid **weekends and national holidays** if you prefer a quieter atmosphere. Local tourists from Luwuk often visit on these days[](https://sg.trip.com/moments/poi-danau-paisu-pok-luk-panenteng-138006275/#1).

### Where to Stay

There are several accommodation options around Paisu Pok Lake:

**Homestays in Luk Panenteng.** This is the most practical choice. **Paisu Pok Luk Homestay** offers simple rooms with a fan and shared bathroom, priced at around **150,000–250,000 IDR** per night[](https://radarlawu.jawapos.com/wisata/2206517929/lagi-liburan-di-sulawesi-tengahtepatnya-di-danau-paisu-pok-lukrekomendasi-penginapan-nyaman-dekat-danau-paisu-pok-luk-untuk-liburan-tenang). **Novpitri Homestay** is the most frequently mentioned name by foreign guests and serves as the default choice for many travelers[](https://luwukbanggai.com/penginapan-kepulauan-banggai/). Expect around **200,000 IDR** for an air-conditioned room and about **150,000 IDR** for a fan room[](https://luwukbanggai.com/penginapan-kepulauan-banggai/). Dinner is usually included or can be ordered at a very low price[](https://luwukbanggai.com/penginapan-kepulauan-banggai/).

**Inns/Guesthouses in Salakan.** If you want more complete facilities, Salakan (the capital of Banggai Kepulauan) is located about **1–1.5 hours** from the lake. Hotel options are more varied, priced at around **250,000–400,000 IDR** per night[](https://radarlawu.jawapos.com/wisata/2206517929/lagi-liburan-di-sulawesi-tengahtepatnya-di-danau-paisu-pok-lukrekomendasi-penginapan-nyaman-dekat-danau-paisu-pok-luk-untuk-liburan-tenang).

**Hotels in Luwuk.** If you have not crossed over to Peleng yet, Luwuk has a more comprehensive selection of hotels. However, staying on Peleng—especially in Luk Panenteng—gives you a major advantage: you can reach the lake early in the morning before day visitors arrive[](https://luwukbanggai.com/penginapan-kepulauan-banggai/).

### How Long Should You Stay?

To enjoy Paisu Pok Lake without rushing, **staying at least one night in Luk Panenteng** is highly recommended. This allows you to visit the lake in the morning, enjoy the village atmosphere, and avoid the risk of fatigue from a long journey. If you visit only as a day trip from Luwuk, you will spend most of your time traveling and have limited time at the lake.

### Practical Tips for First-Time Visitors

**Confirm ferry schedules before leaving.** The Luwuk–Leme-Leme ferry schedule can change, and there is no online booking. Contact a homestay or travel provider for the latest information.

**Prepare physically for the trip.** Traveling to Paisu Pok requires a combination of flights, sea crossings, and overland travel. Make sure you are in good physical condition.

**Bring sufficient cash.** ATMs on Peleng are extremely limited. Withdraw cash in Luwuk before crossing over.

**Respect the local community.** Luk Panenteng is a small village with a slow-paced daily life. Be polite and avoid making disruptive noise.

**Consider using a local guide.** If you do not speak Indonesian, a local guide can assist with navigation, communication, and arranging transportation.

### Is Paisu Pok Lake Worth the Effort?

The journey to Paisu Pok Lake is certainly not instant. You need to fly to Luwuk, cross by ferry or speedboat, and then travel overland to the village. Not to mention limited facilities, scarce ATMs, and ferry schedules that are not always on time.

However, for travelers who appreciate **pristine natural beauty, extraordinarily clear water, and a travel experience that feels like an adventure**, Paisu Pok Lake is one of the most rewarding places in Sulawesi. This lake is not just a photo spot—it is a reminder that some of the best places in Indonesia still require effort to reach.

## 8. FAQ

### What should I know before visiting Paisu Pok Lake?

Paisu Pok Lake is located on Peleng Island, Banggai Kepulauan. You need to fly to Luwuk, cross by ferry (2 hours) or speedboat (1 hour) to Peleng, then take an overland journey of about 1 hour to Luk Panenteng Village. Bring cash, prepare physically, and confirm ferry schedules before departing.

### How much does it cost to visit Paisu Pok Lake?

Entrance tickets are around 5,000–10,000 IDR per person. Motorcycle parking is around 3,000–5,000 IDR, car parking around 10,000 IDR. Canoe rental is around 30,000 IDR, paddleboard around 100,000 IDR, and snorkeling gear around 50,000 IDR. Prices are subject to change.

### Is Paisu Pok Lake safe for swimming?

The lake has a depth of 5–15 meters in several areas. Swimming is allowed, but ensure you are able to swim at such depths. The water is calm and clear. Use a life jacket if you are not confident.

### What should I not do at Paisu Pok Lake?

Do not use soap or any chemical products in or on the edge of the lake. Avoid applying sunscreen or insect repellent right before entering the water. Pack out your trash and respect the surrounding environment.

### Can I visit Paisu Pok Lake as a day trip from Luwuk?

It is possible, but not recommended. The journey from Luwuk to Paisu Pok takes a total of about 3–4 hours one way (including ferry and overland travel). Staying at least one night in Luk Panenteng gives you a much more fulfilling experience.

### Where should I stay near Paisu Pok Lake?

Homestays in Luk Panenteng Village are the most practical option, priced at around 150,000–250,000 IDR per night. Novpitri Homestay is the most frequently recommended name. For more complete facilities, you can stay in Salakan, about 1–1.5 hours from the lake.
