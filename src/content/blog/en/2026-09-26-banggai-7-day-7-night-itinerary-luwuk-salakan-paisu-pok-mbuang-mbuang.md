# Banggai 7-Day 7-Night Itinerary

If you want to explore **Luwuk and the Banggai Islands** without rushing, this 7-day, 7-night route combines road travel, ferries, beaches and remote marine destinations. It is designed for a private trip covering **Paisu Pok, Mbuang-Mbuang, Bontolan, Oyama, Long Beach, Pulo Dua, Kamumu and Salodik**.

> Ferry and marine activities remain subject to weather, sea conditions and local operating schedules.

## Route at a Glance

| Day | Route | Main focus | Overnight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Luwuk → Piala → Salakan | Arrival and island crossing | Salakan |
| 2 | Salakan → Paisu Pok → Paisu Batango → Kawalu → Poganda | Lake and beaches | Salakan |
| 3 | Salakan → Tebing → Mbuang-Mbuang → Bontolan | Jellyfish and coast | Salakan |
| 4 | Salakan → Tebing → Oyama → Long Beach → Bontolan | Beaches and coast | Salakan |
| 5 | Pompon → Mandel → Salakan → Luwuk | Beaches and return ferry | Luwuk |
| 6 | Luwuk → Pulo Dua | Island hopping and snorkeling | Luwuk |
| 7 | Luwuk → Kamumu → Salodik → Luwuk | Waterfalls | Luwuk |

## Day 1 — Luwuk → Piala Waterfall → Salakan

**11:30** arrive at Luwuk Airport, pickup and luggage collection.  
**12:00–13:00** drive to Piala Waterfall.  
**13:00–14:00** visit Piala Waterfall.  
**14:00–14:45** lunch.  
**14:45–15:30** transfer to the port.  
**15:30–16:00** boarding.  
**16:00** target ferry departure from Luwuk to Salakan.  
**±20:00–20:30** arrive in Salakan, transfer and check-in.  
**21:00** dinner and rest.

**Overnight:** Sidapore / Wisma Alfren, subject to room availability.

**12:30–13:30** Paisu Batango.

**15:00–16:30** Poganda Beach.

## Practical Notes

Banggai is an island destination where connections are less predictable than in mainstream tourist destinations. Allow extra time for ferry changes, sea conditions and road travel. Carry cash, drinking water, sun protection and a dry bag for marine activities.
