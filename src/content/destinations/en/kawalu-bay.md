## Kawalu Bay

Kawalu Bay is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### The Beauty of the Curved Bay

Kawalu Bay presents a calm water landscape, protected by curved coral hills. The waters in this bay are very clear and hold marine park potential waiting to be explored by divers.
