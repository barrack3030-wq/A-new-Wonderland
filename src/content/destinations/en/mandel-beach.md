## Mandel Beach: Peleng Island's Quiet White-Sand Shore

Mandel Beach is a white-sand beach on **Peleng Island in the Banggai Islands Regency, Central Sulawesi**. It runs for roughly **2 kilometres** along the island's coast, with fine pale sand, clear turquoise water, green grassy hills behind it, and palm trees along the edge. It is one of the most frequently praised beaches in the Banggai archipelago and one of the least developed.

Sources describe its exact position slightly differently. Some place it in **Kombutokan, Totikum District**; others describe it as between **Kombutokan and Palam**, or in **Desa Palam, Tinangkung Utara**. All agree that the beach is on Peleng Island and can be reached from **Salakan**.

## The Beach and the Landscape

Mandel is a long, open beach rather than a small cove. The sand is **white and fine**, the water shifts from pale turquoise in the shallows to deeper blue further out, and the backdrop is a mix of **grassy hills and coconut palms**. The shoreline catches sunrise light directly, which makes early visits and overnight camping especially appealing.

The beach has **no formal facilities**. A 2025 design study noted that the Mandel area did not yet have facilities and proposed a future plan including a swimming area, jetty, resort, dining area, playground and minimarket. Those facilities are not part of the current beach experience. Some **gazebos** have been reported as abandoned. There are no toilets, food stalls or formal waste management, and visitors have reported litter in some areas. Bring your own trash bag and take your waste with you.

## Swimming, Snorkelling, and Nearby Dive Sites

Swimming is the main activity. The water is generally calm in normal conditions, while **snorkelling and diving** are also possible. Local sources identify **Pulau Liwutung** and **Pulau Sambi** as nearby sites with coral and reef fish. Fishing from shore or with a local boat is also possible.

## How to Get to Mandel Beach

There are two main approaches.

**By road from Salakan:** If you are already on Peleng Island, Mandel can be reached by vehicle from Salakan in roughly **45 minutes to 1 hour**. Sources describe the road as passable by car and motorbike.

**By boat from Banggai:** Some sources describe a boat crossing from **Banggai port** taking around **1–2 hours**, depending on sea conditions. This option is more relevant for travellers coming from the Banggai Laut side.

A small **entrance fee of around 10,000 IDR per person** is documented. There are no fixed opening hours, so morning and daytime visits are recommended.

## Camping and Sunrise

Mandel is well suited to **camping** because the shoreline is open and the beach remains largely undeveloped. There is no organised campground, so visitors must bring their own tent, food, water and supplies.

The beach is also known for its **sunrise views**, which can be seen directly from the sand. Camping overnight gives travellers the chance to be on the beach before sunrise without needing an early transfer.

## What to Bring and What to Expect

**Bring:** Drinking water, food, a trash bag, sun protection, snorkelling equipment, camping gear if staying overnight, and cash for the entrance fee.

**Do not expect:** Toilets, showers, food vendors, fully maintained gazebos, or organised waste management. Mandel is a **bring-everything beach** where visitors need to be self-sufficient.

## FAQ

### Is Mandel Beach the same as Pantai Mandel in Banggai Laut?

Sources use the name Mandel in more than one place. The beach described here is the **Peleng Island beach in Banggai Kepulauan**, reached from Salakan. A village named Mandel also exists in Banggai Laut.

### Can you reach Mandel Beach by road?

Yes. From **Salakan**, the drive takes roughly **45 minutes to 1 hour**. The route is described as passable by car and motorbike.

### Is there an entrance fee?

Yes, around **10,000 IDR per person**, although rates can change.

### Are there facilities at Mandel Beach?

No formal facilities are available. There are no toilets or food stalls, and some gazebos have been reported as abandoned. Bring everything you need.

### Is Mandel Beach good for snorkelling?

Yes. Snorkelling is possible from the beach, and nearby **Pulau Liwutung** and **Pulau Sambi** are mentioned as reef sites.

## Related Destinations

**Paisu Pok Lake** on Peleng Island is the region's signature freshwater attraction and can be combined with Mandel on a Peleng itinerary. **Paisu Batango Spring** and **Poganda Beach** are also on Peleng. On Banggai Island, **Bontolan Beach** offers strong snorkelling opportunities, while **Long Beach** and **Oyama Beach** are boat-access beaches on the Banggai Laut side. For travellers based in Luwuk, **Kilo 5 Beach** is an accessible coastal option.
