## Pompon Beach: Peleng Island’s Free Village Beach and Snorkeling Shore

Pompon Beach is a white-sand beach in **Desa Lalong, Kecamatan Tinangkung Utara, Kabupaten Banggai Kepulauan, Central Sulawesi**, on **Peleng Island**. It is also written as **Pantai Ponpon** and sometimes called **Bone Pompon**. The beach sits approximately **90 kilometers from the regency capital Salakan**, a journey of about **2.5 hours by road** through the hills of Peleng. It was formally inaugurated as a village tourism destination in **December 2022** by the Regent of Banggai Kepulauan and is managed by the village government through its BUMDes.

The beach is known for its **fine white sand, long shoreline, and clear water**—but its real draw is underwater. The **coral reef begins close to shore**, and the water is clear enough for snorkeling and diving with good visibility. It is one of the more accessible snorkeling beaches on Peleng Island, and the destination is listed among Banggai Kepulauan’s featured attractions. citeturn306743search12turn306743search14

## The Beach and the Shoreline

Pompon is a **long, gently curving beach** backed by a mix of coconut palms and ketapang trees. The sand is **soft and white**, the water shifts from pale turquoise in the shallows to deeper blue further out, and the shoreline is **calm and sloping**, making it suitable for swimming and wading.

The beach has simple **wooden seating facing the sea**, and a few **photo spots** have been set up by the local manager, including one called **Sandal Jodoh**. Food stalls operated by village residents sell snacks and drinks, and local cooks prepare simple meals on request.

The beach is **not a wilderness**. It is a village destination used by local families and weekend visitors. During holidays it can become busy, while weekdays are generally quieter. Local tourism authorities reported that visitor numbers at Pompon increased sharply during the 2025 Eid holiday period. citeturn306743search0

## Snorkeling and Diving at Pompon

The main reason to visit Pompon is the **reef**. It starts just offshore and is described as **still healthy**, with **hard and soft corals** and a variety of **tropical reef fish** in shallow water. Visibility is reported as good to very good, particularly in the dry season.

**Snorkeling** can be done directly from the beach. **Diving** is also possible; the reef slopes gently and can suit beginner divers, although there is no full dive center on site. Travelers should bring their own equipment or arrange gear through a local guide or tour operator in Luwuk or Salakan.

**Boat rides** and **water bicycles** are available through local operators, offering a different view of the coastline. Fishing and camping are also recognized uses of the destination in a 2025 academic assessment of its tourism suitability and carrying capacity. citeturn306743search5

## Facilities and Fees

Pompon Beach is described in recent destination sources as having **no official entrance fee and no formal opening hours**. However, current visitor reports indicate that local services and facilities can vary, so travelers should confirm arrangements when they arrive.

Facilities are **simple and village-run**:

- **Wooden seating** facing the sea
- **Photo spots**, including Sandal Jodoh
- **Food stalls** selling snacks, drinks and simple meals
- **Parking area**

Recent visitor accounts also mention **toilets, gazebos and showers**, although water availability can vary seasonally. Bring your own snorkeling and diving gear rather than relying on on-site rental. citeturn306743search2

## How to Get to Pompon Beach

Pompon is on **Peleng Island**, in Banggai Kepulauan Regency.

**By road from Salakan:** From the regency capital **Salakan**, the drive to Desa Lalong is approximately **2.5 hours** according to the supplied travel research, over roughly **90 kilometers**. A car or motorbike is the practical option for the final road journey.

**By boat from Luwuk or Banggai:** Travelers can also reach Peleng Island by boat and continue by road to Pompon. Some tour itineraries combine Pompon with **Paisu Pok Lake** and **Poganda Beach**.

Because transport schedules and sea conditions can change, travelers should confirm current ferry or speedboat arrangements before departure.

## Where to Stay

There is **no formal accommodation at Pompon Beach itself**. Travelers can look for **homestays in nearby villages**, including Lalong, or use guesthouses and homestays in Salakan or other established tourism areas on Peleng.

The supplied research also notes **homestays in Luk Panenteng** near Paisu Pok and basic accommodation in Salakan. Camping may be possible with local permission, but there are **no formal campsites** at the beach.

## Best Time to Visit

The **dry season (roughly May to October)** is the practical period for calmer seas and clearer water for snorkeling and diving. **Morning and late afternoon** offer cooler temperatures and softer light.

**Sunrise and sunset** are both attractive from the beach, while weekdays may offer a quieter atmosphere than holiday periods.

## What to Bring and What to Expect

**Bring:** Snorkeling gear, reef-safe sunscreen, drinking water, insect repellent, a change of clothes, cash for food or local rentals, and a dry bag for electronics.

**Do not expect:** A resort, a formal dive center, guaranteed equipment rental, or full beach infrastructure. Pompon is a **community-managed village beach with developing facilities**. Bring what you need, respect local arrangements, and take your trash with you.

## FAQ

### Is Pompon Beach the same as Bone Pompon Beach?

“Pompon,” “Ponpon,” and “Bone Pompon” are used for the Lalong beach in Banggai Kepulauan. There is also a separate beach with a similar name in Banggai Laut, so travelers should check the village and regency before departing.

### Is there an entrance fee at Pompon Beach?

Recent destination reports describe Pompon as **free to enter with no official opening hours**. Local services or facilities may have separate arrangements, so confirm on arrival.

### Can you snorkel at Pompon Beach?

Yes. The reef begins close to shore and is described as healthy, with good visibility and tropical reef life. Bring your own gear because on-site rental is not guaranteed.

### How do you get to Pompon Beach from Luwuk?

Travelers can take a boat toward Peleng Island and continue by road, or use a longer ferry-and-road route via Salakan. Tour operators may also combine Pompon with Paisu Pok and Poganda in one itinerary.

### Is there accommodation at Pompon Beach?

No formal beach accommodation is listed in the supplied research. Homestays in nearby villages and guesthouses in Salakan are the practical alternatives.

## Related Destinations

**Paisu Pok Lake** is the region’s famous clear freshwater destination and can be combined with Pompon on a Peleng itinerary. **Poganda Beach** offers white sand, calm water and near-shore snorkeling. **Paisu Batango Spring** is another natural attraction in the Luk Panenteng area. **Mandel Beach** is another white-sand option on Peleng. For travelers based in Luwuk, **Kilo 5 Beach** and **Piala Waterfall** are easier mainland additions.
