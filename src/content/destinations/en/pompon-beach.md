## Pompon Beach

Pompon Beach is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### A Quiet Corner of a Tropical Beach

Pompon Beach is a perfect representation of a secluded tropical beach. Its gentle waves combined with a clean coastline make it the best place to read a book or relax to release fatigue.
