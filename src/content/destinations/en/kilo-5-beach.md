## Kilo 5 Beach

Kilo 5 Beach is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Underwater Charm in the City Center

Enjoy the uniqueness of Kilo 5 Beach, which offers beautiful coral reefs right along the main road of Luwuk city. You can go snorkeling immediately or simply enjoy local fried bananas while looking at the calm sea.
