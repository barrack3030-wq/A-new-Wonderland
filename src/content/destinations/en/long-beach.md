## Long Beach

Long Beach is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Endless Coastline

Long Beach Banggai features an extensive stretch of coastline. Perfect for walking barefoot while looking for shells or simply spoiling your eyes looking at the open sea.
