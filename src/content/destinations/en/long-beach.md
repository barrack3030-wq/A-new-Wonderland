## Long Beach

Long Beach—locally known as **Pantai Pasir Panjang**—is a white-sand beach on **Banggai Island in Banggai Laut Regency, Central Sulawesi**. It sits next to **Oyama Beach** and, like Oyama, is **reachable only by boat**, typically from the villages of **Popisi** or **Lokotoy**. There is no road access, no entrance fee, and no formal infrastructure. What you get instead is a quiet, undeveloped shoreline with clear water and direct access to the sea.

Long Beach is commonly included in boat trips around the Banggai Laut coast together with nearby beaches such as Oyama.

## The Beach and the Water

Long Beach is a gently curving stretch of **fine white sand** backed by palm trees. The water is **clear and translucent**, shifting from pale turquoise in the shallows to deeper blue farther offshore.

At one end of the beach, **a single palm leans over the water**, creating a simple and distinctive photo landmark. The beach faces open water, but the bay is partly sheltered, so waves are generally small in calm conditions.

The atmosphere is quiet rather than developed. There are no formal beach facilities, so the appeal comes from the landscape, open water and the feeling of arriving somewhere that is still lightly developed.

## Snorkeling and Marine Life

Snorkeling is possible **directly from the beach** when sea conditions are calm. The reef is not described as being as rich as Pantai Bontolan, but coral and reef life can be found close to shore.

Because rental options on Banggai Island can be limited, travelers planning to snorkel should bring their own mask, fins and basic swimming equipment.

A practical seasonal warning is important. **Jellyfish may appear around the end of March** near Long Beach and nearby beaches. This is not described as a year-round problem, but it is worth asking local residents about current sea conditions before entering the water. One visitor also reported **sea lice** as an occasional nuisance.

## How to Visit Long Beach

Long Beach is **not accessible by road**. The usual approach is to arrange a **local boat** through your accommodation or with a boatman in **Popisi** or **Lokotoy**.

Most visitors include Long Beach in a **boat day** that also stops at **Oyama Beach**, and sometimes **Tanjung Gundul** or **Teluk Lalong**. This makes Long Beach easier to experience as part of a coastal itinerary rather than as a standalone destination.

Boat prices and departure times are not fixed and can change with weather, fuel costs and local availability. **Agree the price before leaving** and confirm with your host what is currently possible. The crossing from Popisi or Lokotoy is short, at roughly **15 minutes in a small boat**, according to the destination research.

## Seasonal Cautions

The main seasonal issue is **jellyfish**, particularly around the **end of March**. If you plan to visit during that period, ask locally before swimming and consider long-sleeved swimwear and swimming shoes if conditions require them.

**Sea lice** have also been mentioned by an individual visitor. They are described as an irritation rather than a serious hazard, but travelers should still pay attention to local sea conditions and leave the water if irritation occurs.

## FAQ

### Is Long Beach the same as Oyama Beach?

No. They are **two separate beaches next to each other**. Both are reached by boat from Popisi or Lokotoy and are often visited on the same trip.

### Can you reach Long Beach by road?

No. Long Beach is **boat-access only**. The nearest road access is at Popisi or Lokotoy, where you arrange the boat.

### Is there an entrance fee?

No. Long Beach is **free to visit**. The main cost is the local boat trip.

### When is the best time to visit Long Beach?

The **dry season, roughly May to October**, is the practical period to consider for calmer seas and clearer water. Travelers concerned about seasonal jellyfish may also want to avoid the end of March.

### Is there food or accommodation at Long Beach?

No formal food or accommodation facilities are described at the beach. Bring your own drinking water, food and snorkeling equipment. Visitors commonly use accommodation in **Bone Baru, Banggai town or Lokotoy** as a base for a day trip.

## Related Destinations

**Oyama Beach** is immediately next to Long Beach and is commonly visited on the same boat trip. **Tanjung Gundul** is a nearby limestone cliff with a sea-carved opening, reached by boat and a short swim. **Pantai Bontolan** in Bone Baru offers snorkeling over coral and habitat for endemic Banggai cardinalfish. For travelers continuing to Peleng Island, **Paisu Pok Lake** and **Paisu Batango Spring** are two of the region's signature freshwater destinations.
