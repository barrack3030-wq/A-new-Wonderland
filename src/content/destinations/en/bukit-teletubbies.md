## Bukit Teletubbies

Bukit Teletubbies is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Vast Green Savanna

As the name implies, the hills in Bualemo resemble the scenery in the Teletubbies series. The rolling green grassland offers a very eye-pleasing photography landscape.
