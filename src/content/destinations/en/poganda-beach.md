## Poganda Beach

Poganda Beach is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Quiet White Sands in Peleng

Poganda Beach presents a stretch of clean white sand surrounded by shady coconut trees. Because it is not too crowded, this beach offers a vacation experience like on a private island.
