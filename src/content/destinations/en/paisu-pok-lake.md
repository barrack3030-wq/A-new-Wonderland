## Paisu Pok Lake

Paisu Pok Lake is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Blue Glass in the Heart of the Forest

Paisu Pok Lake is a natural wonder in the form of a freshwater lake with very clear bluish water like glass. You can even see the bottom of the lake and fish swimming freely from a canoe.
