## Kamumu Waterfall

Kamumu Waterfall is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Refreshing Oasis in Bulagi

Located deep in the remote area of Peleng, Kamumu Waterfall presents a panorama of wide and cascading falls. The lush and cool atmosphere dominates, providing maximum refreshment after a long journey.
