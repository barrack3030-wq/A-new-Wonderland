## Pulau Dua Balantak

Pulau Dua Balantak is one of the stunning destinations in Banggai offering breathtaking natural views and unforgettable travel experiences. This place has its own uniqueness that must be visited.

### Panorama of Two Coral Hills

The icon of Pulau Dua is two giant coral hills rising from the seabed. You can climb one of the hills to get a spectacular view of the sunrise and the vast blue ocean.
