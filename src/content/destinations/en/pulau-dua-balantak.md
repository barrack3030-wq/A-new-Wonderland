## Pulau Dua Balantak: Banggai's Island of Savanna Hills and Coral Reefs

Pulau Dua Balantak is a small island off the coast of **Desa Kampangar, Kecamatan Balantak Utara, Kabupaten Banggai, Central Sulawesi**. It is the **marine tourism icon of Banggai Regency** and has also been proposed as a geo-heritage site. The island is known for a distinctive combination of **savanna-covered hills**, **white and black sand beaches**, and **clear blue water** with healthy coral reefs.

The name “Pulau Dua” means “Two Islands,” referring to the small cluster of two landmasses visible from the mainland. The island described here is the Pulau Dua in **Balantak Utara, Banggai Regency**, not the separate Pulau Dua in Banggai Laut.

## The Island Landscape: Hills, Beaches, and the Walking Path

Pulau Dua is not a flat coral cay. Its defining landscape is a series of **rolling, grass-covered hills** rising directly from the sea. A **walking path cuts through the ridge**, creating the distinctive landscape that is often photographed from the water and from the hilltops.

From the higher points, visitors can look across the **blue sea, mainland coastline, Kampangar village and surrounding islands**. On clear days, distant mountain landscapes can also be visible. The combination of dry grass-covered hills and surrounding blue water gives the island a character often compared with Indonesia's better-known savanna islands, although the scale is much smaller.

The shoreline includes both **white sand and black sand** sections. Near the beach, the water is generally shallow enough for wading and swimming when sea conditions are safe.

## Snorkeling, Diving, and the 35 Dive Spots

The underwater landscape is one of the island's main attractions. Travel sources report around **35 dive spots** around Pulau Dua, including **Ondoliang Rock, Nemo Rock, Alibaba and Batu Gong**. The reefs are described as well preserved, with varied marine life and clear water.

**Snorkeling** can be done directly from suitable beach sections. **Diving** is best arranged through a local operator or resort that can provide equipment and divemaster support. There is no reliable expectation of full snorkeling or diving gear rental on the island, so travelers should bring their own equipment or arrange it in advance.

The area is also used for **fishing**, and marine recreation is closely connected to local community tourism.

## Sunrise at Bukit Lukapan

**Bukit Lukapan** is the main sunrise viewpoint on Pulau Dua. Travelers are commonly advised to start the climb at around **5 AM** so they can reach the top before sunrise.

From the ridge, the sun rises over the **Sulawesi Sea**, with the island's grassy hills forming the foreground. Early morning is also the coolest time for the walk and usually offers softer light for photography.

## How to Get to Pulau Dua Balantak

The journey from **Luwuk** to Kampangar takes roughly **1 hour 40 minutes to 2.5 hours by road**, depending on road and traffic conditions. Official Banggai tourism information also lists Pulau Dua as a destination reached from Luwuk via Kampangar, although travel times and road distances vary between sources.

**Step 1: Drive from Luwuk to Desa Kampangar.** The route is approximately **96 km according to the supplied travel research**, with a mix of paved, narrow and rough sections. A vehicle with good ground clearance is practical for the trip.

**Step 2: Take a local boat from Kampangar.** The crossing to Pulau Dua takes approximately **10 to 25 minutes**, depending on the boat and sea conditions. Reported rates are around **Rp350,000 to Rp400,000 per boat**, generally carrying **8 to 10 people**. Sharing the boat can reduce the cost per traveler.

There is no dependable public transport directly to the island, so travelers normally arrange a private vehicle, motorbike, local transport or a tour in advance.

## Where to Stay and What to Eat

Accommodation in the Pulau Dua area is **simple but available**. The supplied research identifies **Rangga Roa-Roa Homestay** as a more established option, with reported rates of around **Rp200,000 to Rp250,000 per night** and rooms offering AC and private bathrooms. Meal packages have also been reported at around **Rp35,000 per person**, often featuring fresh seafood.

Other homestays and simple accommodation are available around Kampangar and the island area, but availability is limited. **Local warung** can provide simple meals, fish and drinks.

## Best Time to Visit

The **dry season, roughly May to October**, is the practical period for calmer seas, better underwater visibility and easier road travel. **Early morning** is best for hiking, photography and the Bukit Lukapan sunrise, while afternoon light can work well for swimming and snorkeling.

## What to Bring and What to Expect

**Bring:** Cash, snorkeling gear, reef-safe sunscreen, drinking water, insect repellent, sturdy shoes for the hill path, a hat, and a dry bag for electronics.

**Do not expect:** Reliable mobile signal, card payments, full gear rental, polished resort facilities or a highly developed tourism complex. Pulau Dua is a **developing, community-connected destination** where visitors should bring what they need and respect local arrangements.

## FAQ

### Is Pulau Dua Balantak the same as Pulau Dua in Banggai Laut?

No. The island described here is in **Desa Kampangar, Kecamatan Balantak Utara, Kabupaten Banggai**. There is another Pulau Dua in Banggai Laut Regency, which is a separate destination.

### Can you reach Pulau Dua Balantak by road?

You can drive to **Desa Kampangar**, but the final crossing to the island is by boat. The crossing generally takes **10 to 25 minutes**.

### How much is the boat to Pulau Dua?

Travel sources report approximately **Rp350,000 to Rp400,000 per boat**, with capacity for around 8 to 10 people. Actual rates can vary with the operator, route and sea conditions.

### Can you snorkel at Pulau Dua Balantak?

Yes. Snorkeling is possible from the beach, and the island is known for its coral reefs and clear water. Bring your own gear or arrange equipment in advance.

### Is there accommodation near Pulau Dua Balantak?

Yes. Homestays and simple accommodation are available around Kampangar and the Pulau Dua area. **Rangga Roa-Roa Homestay** is one option identified in the supplied travel research, but availability should be confirmed before departure.

## Related Destinations

**Paisu Pok Lake** and **Paisu Batango Spring** on Peleng Island offer freshwater scenery. **Poganda Beach** and **Pompon Beach** are white-sand snorkeling destinations. **Mandel Beach** and **Oyama Beach** add boat-access island beaches, while **Piala Waterfall** and **Kilo 5 Beach** are easier mainland options for travelers based in Luwuk.
