# ULTIMATE BANGGAI

**6 Days 5 Nights**

**Luwuk • Salakan • Peleng • Banggai Laut • Kamumu • Salodik • Pulo Dua**

## Overview

Discover the best of Banggai in one unforgettable journey, combining remote islands, crystal-clear lakes, tropical waterfalls, pristine beaches and the spectacular Pulo Dua islands.

This adventure is designed for travelers who want to experience Banggai beyond the usual highlights — with island hopping, swimming, snorkeling, local culture and breathtaking landscapes.
