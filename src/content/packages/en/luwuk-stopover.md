# Luwuk Stopover

**2 Days 1 Night**
_Luwuk City • Salodik_

## Overview

A short but memorable escape for travelers with limited time in Luwuk. Discover waterfalls, tropical landscapes, coastal views and local experiences without the need for a long island journey.

## Day 1 — Arrival & Luwuk Nature Escape

**11:30 AM — Arrival at Luwuk Airport**

- Airport pickup
- Meet and greet
- Short preparation for the tour

**12:00 PM — Start Exploring Luwuk**

- Depart from the airport
- Lunch / local culinary experience
- Enjoy the journey through the tropical landscape

**Afternoon — Piala Waterfall**

- Visit Piala Waterfall
- Explore the surrounding tropical nature
- Swimming and relaxation
- Photography

**Late Afternoon — Luwuk Coastal Area**

- Continue to the coastal area
- Visit Kilo 5 / selected coastal viewpoints
- Enjoy the sunset
- Photography

**Evening**

- Hotel check-in
- Dinner
- Free time
- Overnight in Luwuk

## Day 2 — Salodik Nature Experience & Departure

**Morning**

- Breakfast and check-out
- Depart for Salodik

**Morning–Afternoon**

- Explore the natural beauty of Salodik
- Visit waterfall / natural attractions
- Swimming and relaxation
- Photography
- Local lunch

**Afternoon**

- Return to Luwuk
- Short city sightseeing if time permits
- Transfer to Luwuk Airport
- End of trip
