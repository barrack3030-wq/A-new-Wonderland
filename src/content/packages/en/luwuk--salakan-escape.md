## Luwuk & Salakan Escape

**3 Days 2 Nights**
_Luwuk • Salakan • Peleng_

### 🇬🇧 English

**Overview**
A perfect short escape combining the coastal beauty of Luwuk with the untouched natural paradise of Salakan and Peleng. Discover crystal-clear lakes, secluded beaches, tropical waters and authentic local life in just three days.

### Day 1 — Luwuk Arrival & Journey to Salakan

**11:30 AM — Arrival at Luwuk Airport**

- Meet and greet at Luwuk Airport
- Airport pickup
- Short break and preparation for the journey

**12:00 PM — Transfer to the Port**

- Private land transfer to Luwuk Port
- Lunch / local food stop depending on ferry schedule

**Afternoon — Ferry to Salakan**

- Board the ferry to Salakan
- Enjoy the scenic journey across the Banggai Sea
- Transfer from Salakan Port to accommodation

**Evening — Check-in & Relax**

- Hotel check-in
- Free time
- Sunset / local surroundings
- Dinner
- Overnight in Salakan

> _Ferry departure times may vary. The itinerary will be adjusted according to the actual ferry schedule._

### Day 2 — Crystal Lakes & Hidden Beaches

**Morning**

- Breakfast
- Depart for **Paisu Pok Lake**
- Explore the crystal-clear lake
- Swimming and relaxation
- Photography

**Late Morning**

- Continue to **Paisubatango Lake**
- Enjoy the natural surroundings
- Local village experience

**Afternoon**

- Continue to **Poganda Beach**
- Relax on the beach
- Swimming / snorkeling
- Lunch

**Late Afternoon**

- Visit **Mandel Beach**
- Beach exploration
- Sunset photography

**Evening**

- Return to accommodation
- Dinner
- Free time
- Overnight in Salakan

### Day 3 — Beach Adventure & Return to Luwuk

**Morning**

- Breakfast and check-out
- Depart for **Long Beach**
- Explore the coastline
- Swimming and relaxation

**Late Morning**

- Continue to **Oyama Beach**
- Beach exploration
- Swimming / snorkeling
- Photography

**Afternoon**

- Visit **Bontolan Beach**
- Final beach experience
- Lunch
- Return toward Salakan Port

**Late Afternoon / Evening**

- Ferry from Salakan to Luwuk
- Transfer to Luwuk Airport or hotel
- End of trip
