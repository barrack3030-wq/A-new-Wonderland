# Luwuk & Salakan Escape

**3 Days 2 Nights**

**Luwuk • Salakan • Banggai Islands**

## Overview

A 3-day private trip for travelers who want to connect Luwuk and Salakan without rushing the itinerary. The route combines sea travel, coastal scenery, local food and time to experience Salakan at a relaxed pace.

The itinerary can be adjusted to ferry schedules, weather, sea conditions, and flight arrival or departure times.

## Day 1 — Arrive in Luwuk & Travel to Salakan

**Afternoon**

- Airport pickup in Luwuk
- Transfer to the city and port according to the available boat schedule
- Local lunch
- Prepare for the journey to Salakan

**Luwuk → Salakan**

- Travel by ferry or local vessel according to the available schedule
- Enjoy views across the Banggai Sea
- Arrive in Salakan
- Transfer to the accommodation

**Evening**

- Hotel check-in
- Dinner
- Free time in Salakan

## Day 2 — Salakan & Peleng

**Morning**

- Breakfast
- Depart for selected destinations around Salakan and Peleng

**Possible destinations**

- Paisu Pok Lake
- Paisu Batango
- Poganda Beach
- Other coastal spots depending on conditions and available time

**Afternoon**

- Local lunch
- Swimming or snorkeling when conditions permit
- Photography

**Late Afternoon**

- Return to Salakan
- Explore the town or coast
- Sunset photography when conditions permit

**Evening**

- Dinner
- Overnight in Salakan

## Day 3 — Salakan → Luwuk

**Morning**

- Breakfast
- Check-out
- Free time for local food or a short walk in Salakan

**Afternoon**

- Transfer to the port
- Ferry or boat back to Luwuk according to the available schedule
- Arrive in Luwuk
- Transfer to the airport or hotel

**Trip ends**

The Luwuk–Salakan route involves sea travel. Ferry schedules, travel time and the order of activities may change because of weather, sea conditions, operator schedules and local circumstances.

Destinations can be adjusted according to available time and field conditions.
